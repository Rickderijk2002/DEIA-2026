How we found each other

Individual GET2 and Team Roles results were entered into the shared Find Your Crew spreadsheet ahead of forming this team.

Problemistic search

Rational process

Identify a resource or skill gap, recruit whoever solves it, on pragmatic criteria. More conflict and less smooth functioning, but wider skills and fewer homophily barriers.

Opportunistic search

Social-psychological

Recruit someone you like once a minimum competence bar is met. Smooth early functioning, but homophily can limit creativity and outside information.

Which one actually describes us, and why: confirming at Monday's team meeting
